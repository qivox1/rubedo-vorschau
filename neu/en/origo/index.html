Back to the source.

You have everything organised — your day, your appointments, your expectations. Origo is the one place where nothing needs to be organised anymore. Just your breath, and the stillness beneath it — that is where we guide you with sound: with tones, and the space between them.

What Origo is

A space where the hustle and bustle of the day cannot penetrate.

Origo is the point where time stands still. In our work, it is the fourth dimension: a quiet space in which breath, perception and stillness merge into a guided journey into the depths. A ritual return to one’s true self.

We combine ancient synergies, such as the art of breathing, with new technologies, such as the findings of neuroscience — and with the ability to achieve states of relaxation and inner resourcefulness: for a life lived in complete harmony or for personal development.

At the studio, we call it ‘Spiramen’: mindful, somatic breathing work. Guided sequences that do not explain what calm feels like, but instead guide you directly into states of deep calm and inner strength.

Here, knowledge is not imparted, but experienced.

  • Presence — the quiet centre amidst a busy day.
  • Your anchor — a breathing ritual of calm constancy that accompanies you wherever you go.
  • Two options — personalised one-to-one support with Monica or round-the-clock access via the Concentus library.
  • Tailor-made — sequences for clarity, deep tranquillity or the stillness of the evening.

Breathing knows no ‘Sie’. From here on, we’ll address you informally.

The noise

The day never stops speaking. Not even when no one is left.

You know the feeling: everything’s done, and yet there’s still that faint hum in the background. Thoughts that are already on your next appointment. An inner tension that can’t be pinned down to a to-do list — because it doesn’t reside in your head, but a little deeper down.

Origo does not work against the noise. It creates a space alongside it — and over time, this space grows. This is not an achievement. It is a permission.

Frequently Asked Questions

What you’ll want to know beforehand.

Book an appointment
What happens during an Origo session?

First comes the personal preliminary consultation: your concerns, your goal, your pace. Your session is then tailored during the planning phase. On the day of your appointment, you’ll experience guided breathing exercises, sound and silence — followed by a brief debriefing.

How long does a session last?

Spiramen lasts 90 minutes. The preliminary consultation takes place beforehand and is included in the price; the follow-up consultation takes place afterwards.

Is there an extra charge for the initial consultation?

No. It is included in the price of your session and takes place before your appointment — at the studio, by telephone or online.

Can I take part online?

Yes. Spiramen and Via are available at the Marienstraße studio and online. For online sessions, you’ll need a quiet room, headphones and a stable internet connection.

What is Spiramen?

The personalised one-to-one session: guided breathing exercises, relaxation techniques and self-hypnosis, tailored to your specific needs. 90 minutes, €210.

What is Concentus?

The Sound Library: recorded sessions, available at any time — no preliminary consultation required, just immediate access. A different approach for people who wish to start at their own pace.

Do I need any experience with breathing exercises?

No. The session is tailored to your pace, and you’ll be guided through everything as you go. Anyone who has never practised conscious breathing before is just as welcome as an experienced practitioner.

How do I prepare?

Please arrive as rested as possible and in comfortable clothing that does not restrict breathing. Avoid large meals two hours beforehand; no alcohol and minimal caffeine on the day itself. Further details can be found under ‘Transparency & Preparation’.

How often is it advisable to have a session?

We clarify this during the initial consultation. Some people come for a single session, whilst others work on a particular issue over a period of weeks — that’s what ‘Via’, the twelve-week programme, is for.

Is Origo a therapy?

No. Origo is a cosmetic experience designed to enhance awareness and relaxation. It is not a medical or therapeutic service and is not a substitute for a medical consultation.

Are all enquiries accepted?

No. Every enquiry is assessed personally in advance: your physical and personal circumstances, as well as your specific concern, must be suitable for the method. If they are not, we will let you know and suggest an alternative that is more appropriate. Full details can be found under ‘Transparency & Preparation’.

When is ‘Origo’ not appropriate?

In the case of certain health conditions — such as cardiovascular disease, epilepsy, pregnancy or acute psychological stress — we ask that you seek medical advice beforehand. You can find an overview under ‘Safety & Breathing’.

Safety & Suitability

When caution is advised.

Origo is a profound sensory experience. Your safety is our priority — which is why we always assess your suitability during a personal preliminary consultation before a ritual begins.

  • A quiet place is all you need — whether sitting or lying down, you may close your eyes. Follow your own rhythm; take a break whenever you like.
  • Please do not use whilst driving or whilst carrying out activities that require your full attention.
  • Origo is not necessarily suitable for those with high blood pressure, cardiovascular diseases, epilepsy, during pregnancy, or whilst undergoing treatment for acute psychological distress.
  • We will discuss these points together during the initial consultation — and if in doubt, please consult your doctor beforehand.
