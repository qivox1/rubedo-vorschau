What Cutis is

The dimension of the skin.

Cutis is the Rubedo dimension for skin and beauty. Your skin is not a sponge that absorbs everything — it is an intelligent organ. It filters, protects, regulates and decides for itself what it needs.

This is precisely where Cutis comes in: not with the next formula, but with the question of what your skin really needs — and what it doesn’t. Understanding rather than overcomplicating.

  • Intelligence — the skin as a thinking organ, not merely a container.
  • Clarity — fewer messages, but the right ones.
  • Precision — tailored to your skin, not off the peg.
  • Ihre Haut — gelesen und verstanden.
The age of overstimulated skin

Ten steps, five serums, a new active ingredient every week — and skin that simply won’t settle down.

Acids, retinoids, peptides, fragrances, plus sun, heat and stress: each individual factor may make sense on its own. Together, however, they create a cacophony — the skin feels tight, becomes sensitive, reacts suddenly and looks tired despite a comprehensive skincare routine.

This is not a call for ‘clean beauty’ nor a matter of doing without for the sake of it. It is not about ‘free from’, but about what is right for your skin. Your skin does not need more — it needs clarity.

Minus, sed clarius.Less, but clearer
The Cutis response

We don’t start with a cream.

But with understanding. Not ‘free from something’, but what’s right for your skin — carefully considered, tailored, in the right measure. Three approaches, one principle: quality of the signal rather than quantity.

Cutis · DNA-Hautanalyse

Ihre Haut, gelesen.

Eine Speichelprobe, im Studio abgegeben. Ausgewertet von der Novogenia GmbH in Salzburg, dem Labor, dessen Name auf Ihrem Befund steht. Gelesen werden Gene, die Ihre Haut beeinflussen: etwa im Kollagenstoffwechsel, beim UV-Eigenschutz und beim Zellschutz.

Den Befund lesen wir gemeinsam, Seite für Seite. Danach folgen fünf Tage Arbeit an Ihrem Protokoll. Die Rezeptur Ihrer Pflege folgt Ihrem genetischen Profil, und Ihr Weg entsteht aus Ihrer Woche. Sie werden über Monate begleitet, persönlich von Monica Galletti.

Sie haben bereits einen DNA-Test gemacht? Wir lesen Ihren Report mit Ihnen, unabhängig davon, wo Sie ihn gemacht haben.

Safety & Suitability

So that the skincare is tailored to your skin.

Cutis uses cosmetic active ingredients, facial rituals and gentle renewal. Your skin is our priority — which is why we always assess its condition and suitability in a face-to-face consultation before selecting any treatments.

  • In the event of acute skin inflammation, open wounds, sunburn or recent cosmetic procedures, we will postpone the treatment.
  • Cosmetic formulations and exfoliants contain active ingredients — for very sensitive skin, known allergies or during pregnancy, we tailor the selection and intensity to each individual.
  • The genome-based analysis is intended to provide guidance on cosmetic treatments — it is not a medical diagnosis and is no substitute for a medical consultation.
  • If you have any skin conditions or specific health issues, please consult your doctor if in doubt.
