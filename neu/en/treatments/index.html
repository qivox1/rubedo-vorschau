Activation · Pillar 02

Vigor

Cold and pressure — a language spoken to the body.

In our second pillar, two forces come together in a finely tuned rhythm: the controlled coolness of cryotherapy — down to −196 °C — and the gentle, rhythmic precision of compression. Together, they awaken a sense of freshness, lightness and presence. Activation rather than strain — not a programme designed for peak performance, but a revitalising stimulus for the body and the senses.
