What Vigor is

The dimension of activation.

Vigor is the Rubedo dimension for activation, resilience and vitality. Not a single treatment, but a conscious, minimalist impulse — effective not through promise, but through experience.

This is not about overloading the senses, but about creating a deliberate sense of appeal: a moment in which energy, focus and body awareness are allowed to flow freely once more. The body is not ‘worked on’, but gently guided with respect.

  • Activation — a clear stimulus rather than overwhelming the reader.
  • Resilience — one’s own inner strengths, consciously awakened.
  • Vitality — freshness, balance and a renewed sense of presence in one’s experience.
  • Two forces — cold and pressure, finely balanced.
Inertia

There are times when the body feels sluggish — and even good routines only seem to work to a limited extent.

Your head feels tired, your legs feel heavy, your energy comes in waves. A gentle state of standby — even though you’ve long since wanted to get moving again.

Vigor understands this condition — and takes it seriously without dramatising it. It does not force anything. It gently lifts the heaviness and gets you moving again.

Motus vita est.Movement is life
Vigor treatments

Two forces, many applications.

Vigor has two forces — the cold stimulus and compression. Each manifests itself in several applications — many approaches, one principle. The cold awakens, the pressure stimulates circulation and envelops. Activation, structure, after-effect.

Cold

The cold stimulus

Controlled stimulation down to −196 °C

An intense, clearly measured stimulus that awakens the body — a moment of clear freshness that leaves alertness and vitality behind.

The duration is fixed — three minutes. The intensity level is not: there are six levels, and we choose the first one together. This is because the body gets used to the cold — so that the stimulus remains a stimulus, the next level becomes possible. Why there are different intensity levels →

Compression

Rhythmic · enveloping

Controlled pressure restores the body’s natural flow and relieves the heaviness in tired legs — a sense of lightness that can be felt from one leg to the other.

The body adapts to what it is familiar with — which is why no single approach remains the right one for long. We reassess the pressure, rhythm and direction, as well as the sequence of active ingredients, at every appointment. And, as with cold therapy, we deliberately avoid starting at the upper end.

The aftertaste

What remains once the allure has faded.

What many describe is not a loud effect, but a subtle afterglow: clarity, lightness, an alert, calm mind — and the feeling of being more at one with oneself again.

This moment anchors the experience not in the end of the treatment, but in the body’s memory. A gentle reset that often makes everyday life feel different afterwards.

Recognisability

Who Vigor is intended for.

  • … You achieve a great deal, shoulder a heavy burden — and often feel as though you’re ‘always on’.
  • … You are familiar with heavy legs, a feeling of low energy, and the desire for a fresh start.
  • … You are active and, following exercise or periods of intense activity, are looking for a mindful balance.

What matters is not an ideal state, but recognising one’s own pattern.

Safety & Suitability

So that the experience suits you perfectly.

Vigor works with physical stimuli — the cold is distinct and noticeable, the pressure gentle and rhythmic. Your safety is our priority — which is why we always assess your suitability in a face-to-face consultation before a treatment begins.

  • Cryotherapy is not necessarily suitable for those with cold intolerance or a cold allergy, cardiovascular conditions, high blood pressure or during pregnancy.
  • Presso-massage, leg bandages and body wraps involve gentle pressure and compression — in cases of thrombosis, acute venous disorders or open wounds, we will discuss these treatments with you beforehand.
  • Bandages and body wraps contain nourishing active ingredients — if you have any known allergies or sensitive skin, we will test for compatibility before application.
  • If you have any specific health concerns, please consult your doctor if in doubt.
