Studio & People

Science meets ritual — even in the building itself.

The studio is run by Monica Galletti — who has been working in the international 5-star spa sector for over ten years. Our work is based on certified training, careful research and years of experience with people who take their self-care seriously — and are willing to set aside time for it.

The stucco façade of our building on Marienstraße is over a hundred years old — and for us, it is more than just a beautiful setting. It embodies the same ethos as the care we provide: craftsmanship that stands the test of time, rather than fleeting fashions.

Arrival

Where to park, where to ring the bell.

Marienstraße 58 is in a quiet location. There are parking spaces in front of the building; the entrance is to the side, indicated by a sign. Ring the bell at Rubedo — Monica will open the door herself.

Upon entering the studio, you are greeted by a room with high ceilings and subdued lighting. You set aside whatever you have brought with you, and the time is yours.

Your first appointment

What happens on your first visit.

A calm, initial consultation: your skin, your daily routine, what you’re hoping to achieve. This informs the choice of treatment — and the pace that suits you best.

Each treatment is prepared especially for you before you arrive. We’ll discuss what to do at home afterwards at the end of your session — turning your appointment into a journey of care.

The Rubedo Principle

For us, time is a deliberate space in the calendar.

Not to be treated as a gap.Not as a matter of habit.We create space.

Every appointment is dedicated to the person who has consciously chosen to book it.

We believe in mindful moments. Because wellbeing begins with a decision.

Appointment terms and conditions

Why an hour is reserved.

Every appointment is for one person. We allocate it once, and we prepare for it before you arrive. The sooner you cancel, the sooner the slot can be taken by someone else — and if we are able to reallocate it, we will not charge you.

Individual treatmentsCancellations or rescheduling are free of charge up to 48 hours in advance. After that, we charge 50 per cent of the price.
Guided sessions · OrigoFree of charge up to 24 hours in advance. Thereafter 50 per cent; 100 per cent if no cancellation is made.
Individually tailored programmesFree of charge up to 7 days in advance. We will credit the full preparation fee towards your booking if you book within 14 days.

And if we have to cancel: you will be offered a new appointment or a full refund.
