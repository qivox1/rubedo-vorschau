What Corpus is

The dimension of touch.

Corpus is the Rubedo dimension for the body and touch. Not merely the release of tension, but ritual bodywork — the art of using one’s hands to re-establish a connection that is lost in everyday life.

Hier wird der Körper nicht als Aufgabe behandelt, sondern als Ort empfangen. Aus vier Traditionen entsteht kein Programm, sondern eine Begegnung — Haut, Atem, Zeit.

  • Touch — the language that comes before words.
  • Ritual — time that belongs to the body, not to the clock.
  • Presence — reconnecting fully with your own body.
  • Four traditions — one language, many dialects.
The Distance

There are days when you live entirely in your head — and only become aware of your body again in the evening.

One carries on, functions, perseveres. The body becomes a vehicle that only draws attention when something goes wrong: tightness, tension, a sense of distance from oneself.

Corpus begins right here — not with strength, but with a hand that listens until the body finds its way back to the present moment.

Corpus meminit.The body remembers
The rituals

Four traditions, one language.

Each of these traditions represents a different form of connection — rhythmic, warm, flowing or deep. We’ll discuss which one suits you best.

The aftertaste

What remains when the hands pause.

What many describe is not a result, but a state: the body softer, the breath deeper, the mind still — the feeling of being fully present once more, in one’s own body.

This sense of calm does not end on the treatment table. It becomes embedded in the body’s memory and carries over into the days that follow.

Recognisability

Who Corpus is intended for.

  • … You spend a lot of time in your head and only notice your body when it makes itself felt.
  • … You carry a sense of tension within you that no longer eases once the working day is over.
  • … You long for touch that wants nothing — except to be there.

You don’t need a reason. Sometimes, the desire to reconnect with your own body is enough.

Who will welcome you

Touch is a language — you have to be able to hear it.

I do not work according to a set formula, but rather according to what your body tells me. My hands listen before they guide — and accompany you to where tension can be released and the body finds its way back to itself.

The gentlest touch is the one that imposes nothing.

Safety & Suitability

So that the treatment suits you perfectly.

Corpus uses oil, heat and touch. Your safety is our priority — which is why we always assess your suitability in a face-to-face consultation before a treatment begins.

  • In the event of acute inflammation, fever, recent injuries or skin conditions, we will postpone the treatment.
  • During pregnancy, in cases of venous disorders (thrombosis), cardiovascular conditions or following surgery, we tailor our massage techniques and heat application to each individual’s needs.
  • Massage oils and essential oils (Raindrop) contain nourishing active ingredients — if you have any known allergies or sensitive skin, we will check for compatibility beforehand.
  • If you have any specific health concerns, please consult your doctor if in doubt.

Transparency

Corpus uses massage, heat and touch as cosmetic, relaxing rituals for wellbeing — these are not medical or therapeutic treatments, nor do they promise a cure. If you have any health concerns, please consult your doctor. Accompanying cosmetic products comply with the EU Cosmetics Regulation (EC) No. 1223/2009.
