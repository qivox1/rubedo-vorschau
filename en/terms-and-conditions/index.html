§ 4 Booking, cancellation and rescheduling

  1. Appointments for individual treatments that do not require personalised preparation may be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 48 hours before the agreed appointment time. In the event of a later cancellation or a no-show, 50 per cent of the agreed service price will be charged as a cancellation fee, unless the customer can demonstrate that there was an obstacle beyond their control or that the loss incurred was minimal or non-existent (for example, because the slot could be reallocated to another client).
  2. Individually tailored services require specific preparatory work: prior to the appointment, the sequence of events, breathing and movement guidance, selection of sounds and music, and the materials used are designed and compiled specifically for the client. This preparation regularly takes several hours and is carried out in full before the agreed appointment. It is covered by the preparation fee and is deemed to have been provided once the work is completed; the fee is therefore non-refundable in the event of a subsequent cancellation. As a gesture of goodwill, we will credit the full amount if the session is rescheduled within 14 days.
  3. For treatments prepared individually for a specific appointment, the free cancellation period is 7 days. In the event of a later cancellation, we will charge 50 per cent of the service price, provided the slot cannot be reallocated; the customer reserves the right to prove that no damage, or only minor damage, has been incurred.
  4. For guided one-to-one sessions in the ‘Origo’ programme (breathing and deep relaxation sessions), which are individually prepared and tailored for each client, the following applies: An agreed session may be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours in advance; in the event of a later cancellation, 50 per cent of the session fee will be charged; in the event of a no-show without prior notice, 100 per cent of the session fee will be charged as a cancellation fee. The client reserves the right to prove that no damage, or only minor damage, has been incurred. Clause 2 applies accordingly to the associated preparatory work; 100 per cent of the preparatory fee paid will be credited if a session is booked within 3 days.
  5. In the event of cancellation by Rubedo Spa (e.g. due to the therapist’s illness), an alternative appointment will be offered or the amount already paid will be refunded in full.

§ 7 Genome-based analyses, saliva samples and bespoke products

  1. Purpose and scope. Genome-based analyses are offered exclusively for cosmetic and wellness purposes. They do not constitute genetic testing for medical purposes within the meaning of the German Genetic Diagnostics Act (GenDG) and are not intended to diagnose, predict or treat diseases or health conditions. No diagnosis, assessment of disease risk or treatment recommendation is provided. Medical enquiries should be directed to a doctor.
  2. Legal age and capacity to consent. The analysis is carried out only for individuals aged 18 or over who have the capacity to consent and who give consent on their own behalf. Analyses for third parties, for minors or without the individual being physically present during sample collection are not permitted.
  3. Information and written consent. A personal consultation takes place before the sample is taken: the purpose and scope of the analysis, the type of characteristics assessed, the recipient of the sample, storage and disposal. The analysis will only commence once we have received your written consent. Consent may be withdrawn in whole or in part at any time without giving reasons; the date on which we receive the withdrawal is decisive.
  4. Right not to be informed. You may at any time state that you do not wish to be informed of individual or all results. In this case, the results in question will not be communicated or discussed.
  5. Recipient of the sample and analysis. The saliva sample is forwarded for analysis to our contractual partner, Beauty ID Switzerland AG, Switzerland, and from there to a certified partner laboratory commissioned by them. Rubedo Spa. does not carry out any laboratory analysis itself. The scope, legal basis and transfer to Switzerland are described in the privacy policy.
  6. Storage and destruction of the sample. The saliva sample will be used exclusively for the agreed analysis and will be destroyed once this purpose has been fulfilled. It will only be stored for subsequent, non-agreed analyses at your express request. If you withdraw your consent before the analysis is completed, the sample will be destroyed immediately.
  7. Results and active participation. The analysis describes predispositions, i.e. tendencies. It does not predict a specific outcome for any individual case. No specific cosmetic success is guaranteed; this depends on your individual circumstances and your active participation. The resulting recommendations regarding skincare, nutrition and lifestyle are sensible conclusions, not medical instructions.
  8. Timeline. There is a gap of several weeks between the sample being taken and the report being available; your personal report will then be processed. Duration estimates are approximate. Delays in the laboratory or during dispatch for which we are not responsible do not constitute grounds for cancellation or a reduction in price, provided that the service is delivered within a reasonable timeframe.
  9. Prices and payment. Analysis, bespoke natural cosmetics and individually formulated micronutrients are invoiced before production begins or before the sample is sent off. Two invoices may be issued: one for the studio’s services, and one for the analysis and products.
  10. Right of withdrawal for distance contracts. If the contract is concluded by means of distance selling, Section 5 of these General Terms and Conditions applies. In the case of analysis as a service, the right of withdrawal expires once we have fully performed the service and you have expressly consented to this before the service begins and confirmed your awareness of the expiry of the right of withdrawal (Section 356(4) of the German Civil Code (BGB)). In the case of products custom-made to your profile, the right of withdrawal expires upon commencement of manufacture (Section 312g(2)(1) of the German Civil Code (BGB)). Samples already sent to the laboratory cannot be retrieved; the analysis fee is non-refundable once the samples have been received by the laboratory.
  11. Prohibition of discrimination. The results of genome-based analyses are not disclosed to third parties — in particular not to employers or insurance companies — and we neither request nor accept such information.
