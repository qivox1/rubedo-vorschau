Good to know

What you should know.

How long is a voucher valid for?

Valid for three years from the date of issue — in accordance with the statutory limitation period (§ 195 BGB). Gift vouchers are transferable. Treatment vouchers can be rebooked for another treatment of the same or similar value if required.

Can I return a gift voucher?

Personalised vouchers are excluded from the right of withdrawal in accordance with Section 312g(2)(9) of the German Civil Code (BGB), as they are issued to a specific person or for a specific appointment. However, we always offer the option to rebook for another treatment of the same value.

Can the recipient of the gift voucher reschedule it for a different date?

Yes. We simply ask that you cancel at least 24 hours before your booked appointment. If you cancel at shorter notice, we reserve the right to charge a proportionate amount against the voucher value — this applies only to appointments that have already been firmly booked, not to open-value vouchers.

Can I have a gift voucher issued for several people?

Yes, on request. If, for example, you’d like to give an experience as a gift for two people, we’ll create a group code for you — just get in touch.

Is the voucher also valid for the comprehensive, genome-based skincare package?

Yes. The genome-based comprehensive skincare package (€2,000) can be paid for in full using a gift voucher. Given the scope of the treatment, we recommend a brief preliminary consultation with Monica in this case — so that the recipient is well-informed from the start and isn’t taken by surprise. A brief 15-minute preliminary consultation is ideal for this.
